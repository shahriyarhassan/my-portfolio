RLC Diagram with Automation studio

I created an RLC circuit diagram in Automation Studio with eight operating sequences. While building it, I learned how a relay works and controls a circuit. You can watch my video demonstration in the Videos section. To open and run the simulation yourself, you need Automation Studio.

Delta PLC project

This project presents a ladder diagram for controlling a star-delta motor starter with a Delta PLC. It demonstrates the control sequence for starting a motor in star and then switching it to delta.

Video of RLC Diagram With Automation Studio(1)

Sequence of this Operation

* The relay will active when we press the start push button.
* When relay is active, the First lamp will be on.
* When the relay is not active, the second lamp will be on.

Video of RLC Diagram With Automation Studio(2)

Sequence of this Operation

* When we press the NO push button, The indicator lamp will be on and remain on even after releasing the push button(Latching).
* The indicator lamp will be off when we press the NC push button and remain off even after releasing the push button.

Video of RLC Diagram With Automation Studio(3)

Sequence of this Operation

* When we press the start push button while the selector switch is in "0" possition, the indicator lamp will turn on without latching(Jog mode).
* When we press the start push button while the selector switch is in "1" possition, the indicator lamp will turn on and remain on even after releasing the push button(Latching).
* The indicator lamp will be off when we press the stop push button and remain off even after releasing the push button.

View video

Video of RLC Diagram With Automation Studio(4)

Sequence of this Operation

* Pressing any of the start push buttons will turn on the indicator lamp and remain on even after releasing the push button(Latching).
* Pressing any of the stop push buttons will turn off the indicator lamp and remain off even after releasing the push button.
